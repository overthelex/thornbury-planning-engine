# Thornbury Audit LLP — Audit Programme

2025 edition. Extract for owner-managed and small-group engagements. Confidential; not for distribution outside the firm.

## How to read this programme

Each procedure has an identifier, an area, the assertions it addresses, a level, an hours figure, a scalable flag and an applicability line.

- **Level** is the lowest assessed risk at which the procedure is expected to run. *Mandatory* procedures run on every engagement where they apply, whatever the assessed risk, because a standard requires them. *Baseline* procedures run wherever the area is in scope. *Elevated* and *significant* procedures run when the area's assessed risk is at that level or above.
- **Hours** is the firm's standard budget at baseline extent for an engagement of typical size. Scalable procedures cost more at higher extents; the sample-size table below applies where the procedure quotes it.
- **Applicability** says when the procedure is in scope at all. A procedure that does not apply is not selected and is not counted as an omission.

Sample-size table for scalable procedures where no other table is quoted: baseline 15, elevated 25, significant 40. Hours scale approximately with sample size.

## Revenue

### REV-01 Analytical review of revenue

**Assertions:** completeness, occurrence, accuracy · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 4 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Compare monthly revenue by customer and by job type to the prior year and to budget. Investigate movements above performance materiality or 10%, whichever is lower, and obtain corroborating explanations from someone other than the person who raised the invoices.

### REV-02 Sales cut-off testing

**Assertions:** cutOff, occurrence · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 6 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Select sales invoices raised in the 15 days either side of the year end and agree each to completion certificates, delivery notes or timesheets to confirm the work was performed in the period in which it was recorded. Sample size per the firm's table: 10 at baseline, 20 at elevated, 40 at significant.

### REV-03 Manual and non-standard revenue entries

**Assertions:** occurrence, accuracy, cutOff · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 5 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where revenue is a significant risk or manual revenue entries exist.

Extract every credit to a revenue account that did not originate in the sales ledger or the till (manual journals, adjustments, credit-note reversals). Vouch each above the trivial threshold to supporting documentation and approval.

### REV-04 Contract revenue and stage of completion

**Assertions:** accuracy, cutOff, valuation · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 10 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** Where long-term contracts span the year end.

For contracts spanning the year end, obtain the contract, the payment schedule, and evidence of stage of completion (surveyor's valuation, site diary, certified applications). Recompute revenue and any amounts recoverable on contracts under FRS 102 section 23 and compare to the amounts recorded.

### REV-05 Subsequent receipts testing

**Assertions:** existence, valuation · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 6 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** All engagements with trade debtors.

For debtors above the trivial threshold at the year end, trace to cash received after the year end. For any unpaid at the date of testing, obtain and evaluate management's explanation and the customer's confirmation.

### REV-06 Cash and card takings completeness

**Assertions:** completeness, occurrence · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 5 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where cash or card takings are a material revenue stream.

For a sample of weeks, agree till or terminal reports to the takings recorded and to the bank. Review the full-year series of takings for gaps and for weeks below the trading pattern; obtain the opening-hours record and explain every gap.

## Debtors

### DEB-01 Debtors ageing and recoverability

**Assertions:** valuation, existence · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements with trade debtors.

Obtain the aged debtors listing, agree the total to the ledger, and review balances over 90 days for recoverability. Consider retentions separately.

### DEB-02 Debtor confirmations

**Assertions:** existence, rightsAndObligations · **Level:** significant · **Hours:** 8 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where debtor existence is a significant risk.

Send positive confirmation requests to a sample of customers selected by value and by risk. Reconcile replies; perform alternative procedures for non-replies.

### DEB-03 Other debtors and prepayments

**Assertions:** existence, classification, valuation · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 2 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Obtain a breakdown of other debtors. For each item above the trivial threshold, vouch to support and confirm it is recoverable and correctly classified. Identify any amounts due from directors or connected parties.

## Purchases and creditors

### PUR-01 Purchases analytical review

**Assertions:** completeness, accuracy · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Compare cost of sales and overheads by account to the prior year and to revenue. Investigate movements in gross margin and in individual accounts above performance materiality or 10%.

### PUR-02 Supplier statement reconciliations

**Assertions:** completeness, existence · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 5 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** All engagements with trade creditors.

Select suppliers by value and by risk (new suppliers, related suppliers, suppliers with unusual invoice patterns). Obtain year-end statements and reconcile to the purchase ledger.

### PUR-03 New supplier and approval-limit analytics

**Assertions:** occurrence, accuracy · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 4 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** Where the client's approval control is relied upon or purchases are elevated.

List suppliers first used in the year. For each, review the approval trail and the pattern of invoice values against the client's approval limits; investigate clustering below a limit, invoices split into parts, unusually fast payment, and the same user posting and approving.

### PUR-04 Duplicate and unusual payment analytics

**Assertions:** occurrence · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 2 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements with a purchase ledger.

Run the duplicate payment routine over the full year (same payee and amount within 30 days, references matching after normalisation). Investigate every hit above the trivial threshold.

### PUR-05 Purchase cut-off and unrecorded liabilities

**Assertions:** completeness, cutOff · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 4 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Review post-year-end payments and invoices received after the year end for goods and services relating to the period. Assess completeness of accruals.

## Payroll

### PAY-01 Payroll analytical review

**Assertions:** completeness, accuracy · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements with employees.

Compare payroll cost by month to the prior year and to headcount. Recompute expected cost from average pay and headcount and investigate the difference.

### PAY-02 Starters and leavers

**Assertions:** occurrence, accuracy · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 4 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where payroll is elevated, or where the prior year found leaver or starter errors.

Obtain the HR starters and leavers list. For every leaver, confirm that no payment was made after the leaving date; for every starter, agree the start date and pay rate to the contract.

### PAY-03 Payroll existence: bank to payroll register

**Assertions:** occurrence, existence · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 5 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where segregation of duties in payroll is weak.

For a sample of months, agree every salary payment on the bank statement to the payroll register and to an employee on the HR list. Investigate payments to anyone not on the list.

### PAY-04 PAYE and NI reconciliation

**Assertions:** accuracy, completeness · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 2 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements with employees.

Reconcile PAYE and NI per the payroll records to the amounts paid to HMRC and the year-end liability.

## Fixed assets

### FA-01 Fixed asset additions

**Assertions:** existence, valuation, rightsAndObligations · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** All engagements with fixed assets.

Vouch additions above the trivial threshold to invoices and confirm capital nature, ownership and approval. Recompute depreciation. Extent: additions above performance materiality plus a sample of ten below; do not extend to the whole register without a documented reason.

### FA-02 Physical verification of the asset register

**Assertions:** existence, completeness · **Level:** significant · **Hours:** 12 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where fixed asset existence is a significant risk or the register has not been verified in three years.

Physically verify a sample of assets from the register to the floor and from the floor to the register, including all vehicles.

### FA-03 Impairment and useful lives review

**Assertions:** valuation · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** Where indicators of impairment exist.

Review the register for assets with no activity or obsolete technology; challenge useful lives and residual values.

## Bank and cash

### BNK-01 Bank reconciliation review

**Assertions:** existence, completeness, accuracy · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Obtain the year-end bank reconciliation. Agree the statement balance to the bank confirmation and the ledger balance to the trial balance. Vouch every reconciling item above the trivial threshold and investigate any unreconciled difference regardless of size.

### BNK-02 Bank confirmation

**Assertions:** existence, rightsAndObligations, completeness · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 2 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Obtain a direct confirmation from every bank the client used in the year, covering balances, facilities, covenants, security and signatories.

### BNK-03 Bank statement to ledger matching

**Assertions:** completeness, existence · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 4 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** Where manual journals to bank exist, cash is handled, or the reconciliation shows differences.

Match every bank statement line for the year to a ledger entry by amount and date. Investigate unmatched lines on either side and manual journals that credit or debit the bank account.

## Related parties

### RP-01 Identification of related parties

**Assertions:** completeness, presentation · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Obtain from management a list of related parties and related-party transactions. Corroborate against Companies House filings, the party register, bank counterparties, the payroll and the minutes. Consider ISA (UK) 550.

### RP-02 Terms and disclosure of related-party transactions

**Assertions:** presentation, accuracy, classification · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 5 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where related-party transactions exist.

For each related-party transaction, obtain the agreement, evaluate whether the terms are arm's length, and assess the disclosure required under FRS 102 section 33. For transactions outside the ordinary course, obtain evidence of authorisation.

### RP-03 Directors' loans, advances and personal expenditure

**Assertions:** presentation, existence, rightsAndObligations · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** Where a director's balance exists or existed during the year.

Identify any balance owed by or to a director at any point in the year, including personal expenditure paid by the company. Confirm compliance with Companies Act 2006 s.197 (member approval above 10,000) and the s.413 disclosure. Consider benefit-in-kind reporting. Review repayment after the year end.

## Journals and management override

### JNL-01 Journal entry testing

**Assertions:** occurrence, accuracy, cutOff · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 8 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Under ISA (UK) 240.32, test the appropriateness of journal entries and other adjustments. Define selection criteria (manual, posted or approved by senior management, outside business hours, round sums, unusual accounts, period end) and vouch the selected entries to support. Sample size per the firm's table: 15 at baseline, 30 at elevated, 50 at significant, plus all entries meeting a 'must test' criterion.

### JNL-02 Accounting estimates and bias

**Assertions:** valuation, completeness · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Identify every estimate in the accounts (accruals, provisions, clawbacks, stock valuation, useful lives). For each, obtain management's method and data, recompute or develop an independent range, and review prior-year estimates against outcome for bias.

### JNL-03 Significant unusual transactions

**Assertions:** occurrence, presentation · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Identify transactions outside the normal course of business and evaluate their business rationale, including transactions with related parties and any that appear designed to achieve a reporting outcome.

## Going concern

### GC-01 Going concern assessment

**Assertions:** presentation, valuation · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Obtain management's assessment covering at least twelve months from approval of the accounts. Evaluate cash flow forecasts, facilities and post-year-end trading. Conclude under ISA (UK) 570.

### GC-02 Detailed forecast and covenant testing

**Assertions:** valuation, presentation · **Level:** significant · **Hours:** 8 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** Where borrowing covenants exist or material uncertainty related to going concern is a significant risk.

Recompute management's cash flow forecast, test key assumptions to evidence, recompute every loan covenant from the audited figures, and perform sensitivity analysis on the assumptions that drive headroom.

## Stock

### STK-01 Attendance at the stock count

**Assertions:** existence, completeness · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 8 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** Where stock is material.

Attend the year-end stock count, observe procedures, perform test counts in both directions and record cut-off details. Where the count has taken place without the auditor, attend a count after the year end and roll back to the year-end quantities using the movement records.

### STK-02 Stock valuation

**Assertions:** valuation · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 6 · **Scalable:** yes  
**Applicability:** Where stock is material.

Test cost to purchase invoices and net realisable value to post-year-end sales for a sample of stock lines. For work in progress, obtain the method and recompute from brew records or job records.

## Opening balances

### OB-01 Opening balances on a first-year engagement

**Assertions:** existence, completeness, valuation · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 10 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** First-year engagements only.

Under ISA (UK) 510, obtain evidence that opening balances do not contain misstatements, by reviewing the predecessor's working papers where they exist or performing specific procedures on the opening stock, debtors, creditors, fixed assets and loans.

## VAT

### VAT-01 VAT reconciliation

**Assertions:** accuracy, completeness · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All VAT-registered engagements.

Reconcile the VAT returns submitted to the VAT control accounts and to turnover per the accounts. Investigate reconciling items above the trivial threshold.

### VAT-02 Input VAT recoverability

**Assertions:** accuracy · **Level:** elevated · **Hours:** 2 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** VAT-registered engagements where the prior year found VAT errors or entertaining is significant.

Review expense accounts for costs on which input VAT is not recoverable (client entertaining, cars, non-business) and confirm VAT was not reclaimed.

## Accruals and provisions

### ACC-01 Accruals and provisions review

**Assertions:** completeness, accuracy, existence · **Level:** baseline · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Obtain the year-end accruals and provisions listing. Vouch items above the trivial threshold to post-year-end invoices or calculations. Confirm every prior-year accrual reversed. Consider whether any obligation known to management is missing from the list.

## Completion

### COMP-01 Subsequent events review

**Assertions:** completeness, presentation · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 3 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Review post-year-end board minutes, management accounts and bank statements up to the date of the report for events requiring adjustment or disclosure.

### COMP-02 Laws and regulations

**Assertions:** presentation, completeness · **Level:** mandatory · **Hours:** 2 · **Scalable:** no  
**Applicability:** All engagements.

Under ISA (UK) 250, obtain an understanding of the legal and regulatory framework and inquire of management about compliance, including sector registrations (CIS, NICEIC, alcohol duty, CQC) as relevant.
