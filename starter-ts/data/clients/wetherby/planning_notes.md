# Wetherby Dental Practice Ltd — FY2025 planning notes

Compiled by the senior from the planning meeting at the practice on 17 February 2026 (Raj Patel, Sue Marshall; the senior for us; Ingrid by phone for the last ten minutes) and the engagement file. Paragraphs are numbered for reference.

## Planning meeting, 17 February 2026

[N1] Sixth year as auditors. The practice has two surgeries, two dentist-directors (Raj Patel and Claire Dunmore, 50% each), a hygienist, four nurses, two receptionists and Sue as practice manager. Nothing has changed in the structure since 2019.

[N2] Income is an NHS General Dental Services contract, paid monthly by NHS England at one twelfth of the annual contract value, and private treatment paid at reception by card. Private fees are entered from the practice management system into Xero weekly by Sue.

[N3] Raj said the year was "steady". Private income was up on last year because of the new implant service, which Raj started in April.

[N4] Asked about NHS contract performance, Raj said they had delivered "about 92%" of the contracted Units of Dental Activity (UDAs) for the contract year to 31 March 2026, and expected to end the contract year around there. The contract value is 1,020,000. Sue confirmed that NHS England claws back undelivered UDAs after the contract year end, usually in the following July, and that the last clawback (for 2022/23) had been "about 40,000". No provision has been made; Sue said Moorland Accountants "usually do that at the year end if needed".

[N5] Surgery 2 got a new chair and delivery unit in May, as planned in last year's budget. Paid from cash.

[N6] Staff are unchanged all year. Payroll is run by Sue in Xero; Raj checks the total.

[N7] Sue approves all purchase invoices under 5,000 and posts them herself; Raj approves above 5,000. Raj releases the BACS runs. Sue said the arrangement "has worked for ten years".

[N8] Rent for the premises is paid to Wetherby Health Centres Ltd, an unconnected landlord, under a lease to 2031.

[N9] Cash: the practice keeps a 200 float. Everything else is card. Refunds are made by bank transfer, authorised by a director.

[N10] Raj asked whether the audit fee could be reduced given how little changes. Ingrid said the fee would follow the work.

[N11] Materiality for FY2025 set by the engagement partner: overall 24,000, performance 18,000, clearly trivial 1,200. Fieldwork budget 60 hours. The plan goes to the partner before fieldwork.
