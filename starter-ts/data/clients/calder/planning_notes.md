# Calder Valley Brewing Co Ltd — FY2025 planning notes

First year of audit. Compiled by the senior from the planning meeting at the brewery on 11 February 2026 (Dan Crossley, Kate Bolton; Ingrid and the senior), a call with Hill & Co on 13 February, and correspondence with the bank. Paragraphs are numbered for reference.

## Background

[N1] Calder Valley Brewing was founded by Dan Crossley in 2016 in a railway arch in Hebden Bridge and moved to the current unit at Hebble End Mill in 2022. It brews cask and keg beer for the trade and cans for retail, and runs a taproom on site, open Thursday to Sunday.

[N2] Turnover crossed the audit threshold in FY2024. FY2025 is the first year requiring a statutory audit. The FY2024 accounts were compiled, not audited, by Hill & Co, who remain the company's accountants and post year-end journals. Hill & Co also prepared the FY2024 comparatives in the trial balance we received.

## Planning meeting, 11 February 2026

[N3] Dan said the year was "tough but we got through it". Trade sales grew, the taproom "did well in summer", and the canning line bought in 2024 is now running two days a week. Margins were squeezed by malt prices in the first half.

[N4] The company has a 600,000 loan from Yorkshire Bank drawn in March 2024 for the canning line and the taproom fit-out, repayable over six years at 6%. Kate said the repayments are a direct debit on the 15th and "Xero sorts the split".

[N5] The loan agreement contains two covenants tested annually on the audited accounts: EBITDA to interest cover of at least 2.5 times, and net debt to EBITDA of no more than 4 times. The bank has asked for the audited accounts by 30 June. Dan said "we're fine, comfortably" and did not have the numbers to hand.

[N6] Two 60hl fermentation vessels were bought from Brewtech in August, as planned, to keep up with keg demand. Paid from cash; the bank was told in advance.

[N7] Staff: 18 on the payroll at the year end. Marcus, the head brewer, left at the end of October; Leah has stepped up. Kate runs payroll in Xero from the rota and Dan checks the total before the BACS goes.

[N8] Stock: raw materials (malt, hops, yeast), work in progress (beer in tank) and finished goods (cask, keg, cans). Counted at the year end by Dan and Marcus, on paper count sheets. Dan valued WIP "by rule of thumb, litres times cost per litre". The count was done on 2 January. When the senior noted that Marcus had left in October, Dan said he had come in as a favour, then corrected himself: he had done the count with Kate.

[N9] Taproom: card terminal settles daily to the bank. Cash is counted by Kate on Mondays and banked on Tuesdays. The taproom opened every week of the year except Christmas week. Takings are entered weekly in Xero from the till Z-read.

[N10] Rosie Crossley, Dan's partner, supervises the taproom 20 hours a week and is on the payroll at what Dan described as "the same as we'd pay anyone".

[N11] Dan mentioned, when asked about anything unusual in the year, that he "had some work done at home in the summer, sorted through the company, and he'll put it right". Kate looked at the floor.

[N12] Beer duty is returned monthly to HMRC. The rate changed in February 2025; Kate said the return template was updated by Hill & Co.

[N13] Dan asked whether the audit would "look at every keg". Ingrid explained sampling.

[N14] No written approval policy. Kate approves invoices under 5,000; Dan approves above. Dan releases all BACS payments.

## Call with Hill & Co, 13 February 2026

[N15] Hill & Co confirmed the FY2024 accounts were compiled from Xero without audit procedures. They post depreciation and year-end journals on Kate's instruction. Asked about the loan, they said interest had been "posted correctly last year" and they had not looked at 2025.

[N16] Hill & Co noted that stock in the FY2024 accounts was "Dan's figure" and that they had not seen count sheets.

## Correspondence

[N17] Yorkshire Bank's relationship manager wrote on 20 January asking for the audited accounts and a covenant compliance certificate by 30 June, and noting that the account had gone into unarranged overdraft for four days in September.

[N18] Materiality for FY2025 set by the engagement partner: overall 30,000, performance 22,500, clearly trivial 1,500. Fieldwork budget 150 hours, reflecting the first-year work on opening balances and the stock count that has already happened. The plan goes to the partner before fieldwork.
