# Ashworth Electrical Ltd — FY2025 planning notes

Compiled by the senior from the planning meeting at Unit 4 on 4 February 2026 (Martin Ashworth, Tom Whitaker; Ingrid and the senior for us), a call with Pennine Bookkeeping on 6 February, and the engagement file. Paragraphs are numbered for reference.

## Planning meeting, 4 February 2026

[N1] Martin opened by saying it had been a good year, "the best since Covid", and that the order book for 2026 is already fuller than this time last year. Two of the housing developers have signed framework agreements.

[N2] He then said, unprompted, that the company is in discussions with a national M&E group about a sale in 2026. Heads of terms are not signed. The indicative price is a multiple of FY2025 EBITDA. The buyer's advisers want the audited accounts by the end of April, and Martin asked whether we could "get it done quicker this year".

[N3] On the team: Tom Whitaker joined as finance manager in February 2025, replacing a part-time bookkeeper who retired. Tom raises all sales invoices, approves purchase invoices under 10,000 and prepares the BACS runs; Martin releases them. Tom also has posting rights for manual journals in Xero, which the previous bookkeeper did not.

[N4] Martin mentioned the new vans. They bought a Transit Custom in March; the older vans are now all on the fleet fuel card. Nothing else capital of note, though they have "been buying a lot of test kit".

[N5] Three housing contracts (Calder Valley Housing, Harrogate Homes, Brightwater Developments) span the year end. Tom said revenue is recognised when the payment-schedule invoice is raised, not on stage of completion. Customers hold 5% retentions for twelve months after practical completion. Martin thought the Brightwater phase 3 final account was "agreed in principle before Christmas".

[N6] Staff: 24 at the year end, flat on last year. HR administration (starters, leavers, contracts) is handled by Helen Ashworth, who is not in the office every day. Payroll is run in Xero by Tom from a monthly email from Helen listing changes. Asked about leavers in the year, Martin said "one or two, the usual".

[N7] Pension: the auto-enrolment scheme moved from NEST to a Scottish Widows group scheme in September. Tom said the transfer was handled by the broker and there were "no issues".

[N8] Premises: Unit 4 is rented from Ashworth Property Ltd under a lease signed in 2022 at 8,500 a month. Martin owns Ashworth Property Ltd. Martin said the unit was refurbished in December at the company's cost "since it benefits the business". He did not think it needed a board minute.

[N9] Cash position is strong and there are no borrowings. The 250,000 overdraft facility was cancelled in 2024 as unused. Martin does not expect that to change before the sale.

[N10] Martin said last year's audit "spent far too long on the vans" and asked for shorter fieldwork this year. Ingrid said the scope follows the risk and that we would send the plan before fieldwork.

[N11] A new subcontractor, Wharfedale Site Services, started in July. Tom says they were introduced by a site foreman and are "cheaper than the others". There is no written contract yet; Tom is "getting one sorted".

[N12] Insurance: fleet and public liability renewed in October with the same broker. Premium up 9%.

[N13] Martin asked whether the company should move to quarterly management accounts. Ingrid suggested he discuss it with the buyer's advisers.

[N14] Parking at Unit 4 remains a problem; the landlord (Ashworth Property Ltd) is applying for four more spaces.

## Call with Pennine Bookkeeping, 6 February 2026

[N15] Jane Holroyd has given notice and finishes in April 2026. She keys the purchase ledger from scanned invoices, usually in Friday or Saturday batches, and prepares the VAT return for Tom to submit. Pennine will assign someone else but has not said who.

[N16] Jane said the MISC-OPS code "is still being used for bits and pieces" despite last year's request to retire it. She said Tom "posts a few things himself now" and she does not always see them.

[N17] Jane confirmed the Xero export we received on 2 March is complete "as far as she knows".

## Engagement file

[N18] Fourth year as auditors. Prior-year opinion unmodified. No changes to the accounting system (Xero) during the year.

[N19] The FY2024 management letter raised the director's loan approval, the leaver process and the duplicate-invoice check. Management responded in writing that all three were addressed.

[N20] Materiality for FY2025 set by the engagement partner: overall 50,000, performance 37,500, clearly trivial 2,500. Fieldwork budget 120 hours across the team. The plan goes to the partner for approval before fieldwork starts.
